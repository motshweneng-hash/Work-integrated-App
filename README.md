Group 9 App

Adventure Escape SA is a simple one-page booking website for outdoor adventures in the Western Cape. The website helps visitors discover experiences, view adventure packages, calculate their fees, and submit a booking all in one place. Its message is that today is a good day to go camping, and it invites everyone to find their kind of adventure.The website offers seven packages, which are the Ultimate Adventure Day at R850, the Family Explorer Package at R500, the Ziplining Adventure at R750, the Mountain Adventure Package at R1000, the Kayaking Experience at R650, the Rock Climbing Session at R700, and the Corporate Team Challenge at R950, all per person. Visitors can click View Details to see what each adventure includes, then use the Calculate Fees form to enter their details, choose their packages, and see the total update automatically. The total includes a 10% group discount for five or more guests, plus 15% VAT.

The vision of Adventure Escape SA is to create unforgettable escape experiences that are safe, inspiring, and accessible to everyone, and that accommodate every age group, from young children and families to teenagers, adults, and older visitors. The mission is to deliver high-quality adventure activities focused on teamwork, safety, fun, and sustainable travel. The goals are to expand to new locations by 2027, provide successful guided adventure tours, and promote eco-friendly outdoor practices.The website is built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript only. To run it, keep index.html, style.css, script.js, and logo.png in the same folder and open index.html. To publish it, upload the files to GitHub and turn on GitHub Pages, or drag the folder onto Netlify Drop.
